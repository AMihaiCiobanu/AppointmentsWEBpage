Financial reports

Know your numbers. Make better decisions.

The app works like a personal financial analyst for your business — without the cost or complexity.

Monthly and yearly breakdown: revenue (estimated vs actual), expenses, and net profit — with comparisons vs. last month and the same month last year.

Track how many hours and days you've actually worked.

Identify your most valuable clients by visit frequency.

See exactly how many no-shows you've had and how much income they've cost you.

Security & privacy

Your data stays yours. Always.

Your client list and financial records are sensitive. Everything is designed to give you complete peace of mind.

Face ID & Fingerprint: nobody can access your reports or client list if you leave your phone on the table.

Cloud backup & sync: your data is automatically backed up. Export or import everything as a secure JSON file anytime.

Fully localised: 10 languages including English, Romanian, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, Italian, Bulgarian, Polish, Turkish and Russian — with 20 currencies from RON and EUR to BRL, TRY and UZS.
