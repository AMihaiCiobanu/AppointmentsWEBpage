Features

Everything you need, built for how you actually work

All your tools in one place — easy to reach even when you're with a client. Sign in instantly with Google, no passwords to remember.

Smart appointment calendar

See who's coming, when, and for what service. Suggests your next free slot automatically, prevents double-bookings, handles recurring days off and vacations.

Full client history at a glance

All contact info and visit history in one place. Add notes, flag unreliable clients, and get automatic birthday reminders so you never miss a personal touch.

Revenue tracked automatically

Every appointment creates an income entry. Add expenses in one tap. Your daily profit is calculated instantly — no spreadsheets, no guessing.

4-mode calendar view

Switch the same calendar between four views: availability, income, expenses, and profit. See your business performance day by day at a glance.

Confirmations via SMS & WhatsApp

Send appointment confirmations in one tap. Templates ready to go — date, time, and your social links inserted automatically.

Client subscriptions & passes

Offer session-based or monthly subscriptions. Each appointment deducts a session automatically — no manual counting, no guesswork.

Group classes

Set up a class with its own schedule and repeat pattern — the app generates every session for you. Cap the seats, enrol clients, and let them book a place through your booking link; a full class shows as full.

Lock screen widget

Your next appointment on your iPhone lock screen via Live Activities — client name, service, and time, without even unlocking your phone.

Online Booking

Share a booking link with your clients — they pick a service, date, and time. Requests land in your app, you approve with one tap. No double-bookings, no back-and-forth.
