What happens after the 7-day trial?

On both Android and iOS, you start on the Free plan — no payment method, no expiry. When you subscribe to Pro, your first 7 days are free; you can cancel anytime within those 7 days under Google Play / App Store rules and you won't be charged. If you cancel or your subscription ends, the app simply stays on the Free plan — nothing disappears.
