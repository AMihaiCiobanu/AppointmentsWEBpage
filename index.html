Inside the app

Take a tour of the app, screen by screen

These are real screenshots — exactly what you'll see on your phone the moment you start using it.

Calendar

Your whole day on one screen

The month on top, today's schedule below. The colours tell you how busy each day is before you even tap on it.

  • Free time left
  • Almost fully booked
  • Vacation
  • Call, text or WhatsApp a client straight from their appointment.
  • Breaks, personal time and reminders sit in the same timeline as your appointments.
  • Group classes show how many seats are already taken.
  • You decide when a day turns red — set your own free-minutes threshold.
Services

Standard, subscription or class — you choose

Each service has its own price and duration, and they're grouped by type so your list stays tidy.

  • Standard services for one-off appointments.
  • Subscriptions — session packs or monthly passes that count down on their own.
  • Group classes with their own schedule and a seat limit (Pro).
  • Share a single service or your whole price list in one tap (Pro).
Clients

Know every client before they walk in

Open any client and the whole story is there — how often they come, how much they've spent and what's left on their pass.

  • Visits, total spent, last visit and cancellations at the top.
  • “Comes back about every 21 days” — the app works out each client's rhythm for you.
  • Active passes with remaining sessions and expiry date, plus upcoming and past appointments.
Online booking Pro

Booking requests land straight in the app

Clients book from your link, but you stay in control: approve or reject every request with one tap.

  • Create several booking links and switch each one on or off whenever you want.
  • Clients only see free slots, based on your working hours.
  • Make a private link for one client and turn it off once they've booked.
Reminders Pro

Bills and tasks, never forgotten

Add a reminder with a date and time and choose how often it repeats — daily, weekly, monthly or yearly.

  • Overdue reminders show up in red at the top of your day until you tick them off.
  • Perfect for rent, electricity, supply orders or anything that comes back every month.
Settings

Set it up once, the way you work

Tell the app what your week looks like and it takes care of the rest — from free slots to your monthly target.

  • Working hours for each day of the week, plus your vacations.
  • A monthly income target — reports show how each month compares.
  • Your own SMS and confirmation texts, with the date and time filled in automatically.
  • Light, dark or system appearance, 10 languages and 20 currencies.

Financial reports Pro

Know your numbers. Make better decisions.

The app works like a personal financial analyst for your business — without the cost or complexity.

  • Monthly and yearly breakdown: revenue (estimated vs actual), expenses, and net profit — with comparisons vs. last month and the same month last year.
  • Set a monthly target and see at a glance whether each month beat it.
  • Rank your services by income or by income per hour — and see which one is the most productive.
  • Track how many hours and days you've actually worked.
  • Identify your most valuable clients by visit frequency.
